# Privacy Policy for Next Meteo

**Last updated:** May 20, 2026

Thank you for using **Next Meteo**. Your privacy is extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our web application.

---

## 1. No Personal Data Collection

Next Meteo is built as a minimalist, privacy-first hobby project. **We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.**

- We do not have user accounts.
- We do not track your search history.
- We do not collect your IP address or device identifiers.

---

## 2. Geolocation Data

If you use the "Current Location" feature, the app requests access to your device's geographical coordinates via the browser's native Geolocation API.

- This data is **processed purely on the client side** (your device) to fetch the weather for your current area.
- Your location coordinates are sent directly to the weather provider ([Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/)) to retrieve the forecast.
- We **never** store or log your location on any server.

---

## 3. Cookies and Local Storage

Next Meteo does not use tracking, advertising, or third-party analytics cookies. We only use local browser storage for essential, functional purposes:

- **Functional Cookies / LocalStorage:** We use these solely to remember your UI preferences (such as chosen temperature/wind units, and your "Favorite" bookmarked cities).
- These settings remain strictly on your own device and are never transmitted to us.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

Our app fetches meteorological data from **Open-Meteo**. When the app requests a forecast, your browser communicates directly with their API. Open-Meteo is a privacy-friendly service that does not track users or store personal data. You can review their policy here: [Open-Meteo Privacy Policy](https://open-meteo.com/en/privacy).

---

## 5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect functional updates to the app. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
